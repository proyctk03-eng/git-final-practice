# Chien Luoc Dan Trang: CSS Grid vs Flexbox

CSS Grid duoc thiet ke cho khong gian hai chieu (2D), kiem soat dong thoi ca hang va cot (Layout-First). Vi vay, Grid la cong cu toi uu de dinh hinh khung suon tong the cua trang web, dac biet la bo cuc bat doi xung da chieu nhu Bento Dashboard ma khong can long the phuc tap.

Nguoc lai, Flexbox van hanh theo khong gian mot chieu (1D), phan bo kich thuoc linh hoat dua tren noi dung thuc te (Content-First). Flexbox hoan hao cho cac thanh phan chi tiet (Components) nhu Navbar hoac nhom the noi bo, giup cac phan tu tu dong co gian va can chinh truc quan.

Ket hop Grid o cap vi mo va Flexbox o cap vi mo tao nen giao dien gon gang, thich ung cao va toi uu ma nguon.
